Calibrate gaze

This page goes full screen. First you sit about 30 cm from the screen and line your head up with an outline, then you look at 24 dots, one at a time, until each one moves. It takes about a minute. Your camera turns on when you click the circle, and it stays on until the dots are done. No picture is saved, and the numbers from the run stay on this Mac.
